"I'm not here to help you rank. Ranking is yesterday's game. I'm here to make you the answer AI gives when a buyer asks who to trust."

Krishna Kaanth MFounder and CEO

Everyone tells me AI search is just SEO with a new name. I think they're wrong. SEO best practices didn't die. They became the basics, the floor you stand on. The real game is understanding how these models actually decide what to trust, then earning that trust.

I learned the old game first at a bootstrapped company with no ad budget, where we cracked search and grew it past $2M from pure inbound. Then I watched AI rewrite the rules and started MaximusLabs to play the new one honestly.

  • Most people try to hack the algorithm. Build a brand it can't ignore, and you never have to.
  • Understand the algorithm's goal and your own, and you can do magic.
  • There's no page two in AI search. You're the answer, or you're invisible.

Five things we believe that most agencies don't.

The principles behind every engagement we take on.

  1. 01

    Revenue is the only metric.

    Clicks and impressions are vanity. If a number does not move pipeline, we do not celebrate it.

  2. 02

    Brand beats algorithm.

    Build a brand AI cannot ignore and you stop chasing every update. The algorithm starts chasing you.

  3. 03

    Be remarkable or be invisible.

    In AI search only 5 to 10 brands get cited per question. Purple Cow logic, ported to the AI era.

  4. 04

    Founder's voice or it does not ship.

    After AI flooded the internet with sameness, a real point of view is the only moat left.

  5. 05

    Be stupid enough to try.

    We experiment daily, publish what we learn, and change our minds when the data demands it.

How we work#how-we-work

Positioning first.
Then everything AI reads.

Inbound only: we never run paid media. Every result comes from organic search and AI answers, and every change is measured in Max Visibility, our own AI visibility platform.

  1. Start

    Positioning read

    We sit with your founder and team on ICP, buyer journey and positioning. That becomes the description we want every AI model to give.

  2. Measure

    Baseline in Max Visibility

    Your buyers' prompts run on every major model. We record who gets named, how you're described and which sources decide it.

  3. Build

    Pages and earned mentions

    Our strategists write the pages AI can quote and earn mentions on the sources models already trust.

  4. Every month

    Test, report, repeat

    Every change is measured model by model. We walk you through what moved, what did not, and what we do next.

Questions

What people ask about MaximusLabs before the first call.

What is MaximusLabs AI?
MaximusLabs AI is a Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) and Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) company for B2B SaaS and AI brands. It gets brands cited, trusted and recommended when buyers ask ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and other AI models who to buy from.
Who founded MaximusLabs AI?
MaximusLabs AI was founded in 2025 by Krishna Kaanth M, its Founder and CEO. He is a brand strategist who learned search at a bootstrapped company, growing it past $2M in revenue from inbound alone, before starting MaximusLabs for the AI search era.
Where is MaximusLabs AI based?
MaximusLabs AI is headquartered in Bangalore, India and serves clients across 8+ countries, including the United States, India, Israel and markets in Europe.
What services does MaximusLabs offer?
MaximusLabs offers Generative Engine Optimization, Answer Engine Optimization and B2B SEO, backed by technical SEO, plus agentic commerce optimization for brands that sell through AI shopping agents, and brand strategy work: AI perception audits, positioning and messaging, and AI search experiments measured in its own platform, Max Visibility.
Who does MaximusLabs work with?
MaximusLabs works mainly with B2B SaaS and AI companies, typically between $5M and $100M in annual revenue, and with brands in cybersecurity, HR tech, data, fintech and ecommerce. Clients it can name include Oliv AI, UnderDefense, Explorium, HROne and EasyWebinar; others work with it under NDA.
How is MaximusLabs different from an SEO agency?
MaximusLabs is run by brand strategists, not keyword specialists. It starts from your ICP and positioning, works on what every AI model says about you, and measures the change model by model in Max Visibility, with revenue as the goal rather than rankings or traffic.
Does MaximusLabs run paid ads?
No. MaximusLabs is inbound only. It never runs paid media; every result comes from organic search and AI answers.
How do I start working with MaximusLabs?
