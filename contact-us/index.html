Questions

What people ask before they book.

Is the strategy call free?
Yes. It is 30 minutes with a MaximusLabs brand strategist, and you leave with a read on your AI search position whether or not you work with us.
Who will I talk to?
A senior brand strategist from MaximusLabs, often the founder, Krishna Kaanth M. You talk to the person who would run your strategy, not a sales rep.
What should I prepare?
Nothing is required. If you can, bring your top competitors and two or three questions your buyers ask AI before they buy. We run those prompts live on the call.
Do you work with companies outside the US?
Yes. MaximusLabs is headquartered in Bangalore, India and works with B2B SaaS, AI and ecommerce brands across 8+ countries, including the United States, India, Israel and markets in Europe.
Can I just try Max Visibility?
Yes. Max Visibility, the MaximusLabs AI visibility platform, has its own plans. Pick “Max Visibility” in the form and we will set it up with you. See Max Visibility plans →
