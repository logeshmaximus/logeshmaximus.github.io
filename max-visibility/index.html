What is Max Visibility?

Max Visibility is the AI visibility platform built by MaximusLabs. It tracks how ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Microsoft Copilot, Grok, Meta AI and DeepSeek mention, rank and describe your brand, which sources they cite, and which actions will move those answers. Every MaximusLabs engagement runs on it.

What is AI visibility tracking?

AI visibility tracking measures how often AI models name your brand when buyers ask about your category, where you rank in those answers and how you are described. Max Visibility runs your buyer prompts on every model daily and reports visibility, share of voice, position, sentiment and win rate.

Which AI models does Max Visibility track?

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Microsoft Copilot, Grok, Meta AI and DeepSeek. Plans include a core set of models, and you can add more as your buyers' habits change, so you only pay for the models that matter in your market.

What's the difference between AI visibility and brand perception?

Visibility tells you whether AI names you. Perception tells you how: the attributes it attaches to you, the claims it makes and the objections it raises. Max Visibility tracks both, and fact-checks every claim against your own facts.

How do you choose the prompts to track?

We start from your buyers' words, not your positioning. Prompt discovery suggests prompts by market, persona and buying stage; you pick what to track, add your own, or upload a list. Fan-out queries show what each model searched for before it answered.

How often are answers refreshed?

Every tracked prompt runs daily on every model you choose, and every answer is kept, so you can open any day and read exactly what each model said.

Can Max Visibility track other countries and languages?

Yes. Each prompt runs in the country you set, so you see the answers buyers get in each market, side by side.

Can we get the raw data?

Yes. Export any chart or table as CSV, share saved views as a public link or PDF, connect Looker Studio, use the API, or query your data from Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor through the MCP server.

Do we get a strategist too?

Yes. Max Visibility comes with every MaximusLabs engagement, and a strategist reads it with you every week and turns it into the next page, source or message to change.
