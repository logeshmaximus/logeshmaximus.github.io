In short

What agentic commerce optimization is, in two sentences.

Agentic commerce optimization makes your products the ones AI shopping assistants recommend, and the ones AI agents can actually buy.

MaximusLabs fixes the four things an agent checks, then measures every AI shopping answer that names you.

What an AI agent checks before it recommends you

The MaximusLabs four-layer model
  • Layer 1Find

    AI crawlers reach your product pages, and your feeds are live where each platform reads them.

  • Layer 2Read

    Every product has complete, matching attributes in the feed and in the page's structured data.

  • Layer 3Trust

    Reviews, ratings, returns and shipping an agent can check, plus the sources AI already cites.

  • Layer 4Buy

    A checkout an agent can complete, wherever the platform supports it today.

Feeds an agent
can actually read.

AI shopping answers are built from product data. We audit and fix the feeds each platform reads, so every SKU carries complete, current attributes.

  • Feed audit

    Fill rate for every attribute, by SKU and category.

  • Merchant programs

Pages that match
your feed.

Google checks your product pages against your feed. We add schema.org Product markup with offers, ratings, returns and shipping, and make sure both sources agree.

  • Product and offer markup

    Price, availability, GTIN and variants in JSON-LD.

  • Returns and shipping

    Return windows and delivery times machines can read.

  • Feed-to-page match

    Every mismatch between page and feed, flagged before AI sees it.

FAQ

Questions,
answered.

What ecommerce teams ask us before they start.

What is agentic commerce optimization?
Agentic commerce optimization makes your products the ones AI shopping assistants recommend and AI agents can buy. MaximusLabs fixes what agents check, from product feeds and schema to reviews and checkout readiness, then measures every AI shopping answer in Max Visibility.
How is it different from SEO or GEO?
SEO ranks pages and GEO gets your brand cited in answers. Agentic commerce works at the product level: feeds, product data, merchant signals and checkout. MaximusLabs runs all three from one strategy, because AI uses all three when it picks a product.
Which AI platforms do you cover?
Every major AI model that answers shopping questions, including ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Mode and AI Overviews, Perplexity, Copilot, Claude, Grok and Meta AI. MaximusLabs tracks them all in Max Visibility.
Can AI agents buy my products today?
On some platforms, for some merchants. ChatGPT, Google (UCP, a US pilot), Copilot Checkout and Perplexity Instant Buy each have their own programs and eligibility rules. MaximusLabs checks which ones fit your store and gets you ready for them.
Do I need ACP or UCP?
Most stores don't build either from scratch. Your commerce platform or payment provider often handles the protocol, while you handle the feed, the merchant program and the data quality. MaximusLabs maps what your stack already supports.
Does it work on Shopify or a custom store?
Yes. The work is feeds, structured data, policies and content, which apply to any store. MaximusLabs adapts the implementation to your platform.
How do you measure results?
In Max Visibility: product visibility in AI shopping answers, price and rating accuracy, AI bot visits to your product pages, and AI-referred sessions and orders through GA4. MaximusLabs reports them against your starting point every month.
Do you replace my feed management tool?
No. Feed tools move the data; MaximusLabs decides what the data should say, fixes the gaps and measures whether AI recommends you.
How soon do products show up in AI answers?
It depends on your category, your starting point and how fast fixes ship. MaximusLabs sets the baseline first and shows movement model by model as each change lands, rather than promising a date.
