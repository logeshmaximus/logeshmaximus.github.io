Start where
the money is.

The plan puts the pages buyers read while choosing first: alternatives, pricing, comparisons and "best tool for" guides. Each article is a full guide to one keyword, and every row is checked before you approve it.

  • Buying pages first

  • One guide per keyword

  • No cannibalisation

    Every row is checked against the plan and your live pages, so no two pages chase one search.

Questions

What B2B teams ask us before they start. Something else on your mind? Ask us on the strategy call.

What is B2B SEO?

B2B SEO is ranking for the searches business buyers make while they research and choose a vendor, then turning those visits into demos and pipeline. MaximusLabs starts with buying-stage pages such as comparisons and pricing, and reports results in opportunities, not pageviews.

How is B2B SEO different from technical SEO?

B2B SEO decides what to rank for and what to write. Technical SEO makes sure search engines, AI crawlers and agents can reach and read the site at all. MaximusLabs runs them as separate services, and a site with crawl or indexing problems gets the technical foundation first.

Which pages does MaximusLabs write first?

Bottom-of-funnel pages come first: alternatives, comparisons, pricing and "best tool for" guides, then mid-funnel guides. Each article is a full guide to one keyword, and MaximusLabs checks every plan row against your live pages so no two pages compete for the same search.

How does MaximusLabs measure pipeline from SEO?

MaximusLabs reads conversions from your GA4 events and matches opportunities from your CRM export, so you see which page started a deal and which pages helped close it. AI citations for each article are tracked in Max Visibility.

Do you write for AI answers as well as Google?

Yes. Every MaximusLabs article opens with a short, quotable answer and uses real tables, plain definitions and an FAQ, the structure that lets Google rank a page and AI answers cite it.

Who writes the articles, and in whose voice?

MaximusLabs strategists write each article from a brief you approve. The brief carries notes from your calls, so the article sounds like your team, and every draft gets a scored review before it reaches you.

How long before B2B SEO shows up in pipeline?

It depends on your category, your starting point and how fast pages ship. MaximusLabs ships buying-stage pages first, because they are closest to a decision, and reports rankings, AI citations and opportunities every month rather than promising a date.

What does B2B SEO with MaximusLabs cost?

It depends on scope: how many pages, how much research and which markets. MaximusLabs shares a scoped proposal after a 30-minute strategy call and does not publish prices on service pages.
